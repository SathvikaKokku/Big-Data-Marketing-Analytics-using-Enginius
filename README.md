# Marketing-Analytics-on-Bloomin-Brands-Fleming-s-Steakhouse-

Project Focus:

1.Conducted survey to assess customer preferences and experiences at Fleming’s new flagship location in Tampa while also comparing it with other fine dining Steakhouses to optimize the dining experience and evaluating investment impact.

2.Conducted Segmentation Analysis to identify distinct customer groups and refine menu offerings and marketing strategies based on key dining preferences (steak variety, atmosphere, service quality, etc.).

3.Developed Perceptual Maps to compare Fleming’s with competitors, identifying areas of strength and opportunities for improvement in steak variety, atmosphere, and pricing.

4.Performed Sentiment Analysis on reviews from OpenTable, Yelp, and TripAdvisor, uncovering customer sentiments and actionable insights to address service issues and enhance customer satisfaction.

5.Delivered strategic recommendations for expanding successful initiatives across locations based on customer feedback and competitive analysis.
